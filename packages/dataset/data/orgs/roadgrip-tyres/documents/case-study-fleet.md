# Case Study: Cutting Fleet Tyre Cost per km by 18%

*Fictional company and customer. Synthetic demo content.*

## The customer

**NorthStar Freightways** (a fictional customer) is a full-truckload operator based in the National Capital Region. It runs 2,300 heavy trucks on routes connecting Delhi, Gujarat, Maharashtra and the east coast ports. The fleet is mostly tractor-trailers and multi-axle rigids, and each truck covers about 11,000 km a month.

## The challenge

In 2024, tyres were NorthStar's third-largest operating cost after fuel and driver wages. The fleet team had identified four problems:

- **Cost per km had risen 14% in two years**, driven by higher tyre prices and early removals.
- **There was no pressure discipline.** Spot checks found that 38% of tyres were running more than 15% under-inflated.
- **Breakdowns on the highway** caused an average of 2.1 unplanned stops per truck per quarter, often with loads that could not wait.
- **Retreading was fragmented.** Casings went to a dozen local retreaders of varying quality, and many were scrapped after a single retread.

"We had three people chasing tyre invoices and nobody managing tyre health," said the company's head of fleet maintenance.

## The solution

NorthStar moved its whole fleet onto the **Roadgrip Fleet Cost-per-km Programme** under a 36-month contract.

1. **HaulMax TBR fitment.** Every new tyre fitted was a HaulMax long-haul radial with an embedded pressure and temperature sensor.
2. **FleetCare monitoring.** Sensor readings were streamed into NorthStar's existing telematics platform, with alerts for under-inflation, overheating and slow leaks.
3. **Depot technicians.** Roadgrip placed technicians at NorthStar's four main depots to handle rotation, alignment, pressure correction and casing inspection.
4. **Managed retreading.** Casings were retreaded at Roadgrip's approved facilities, and each casing's history was tracked from first fitment.
5. **One monthly bill.** NorthStar paid a fixed rate per km for each application, so it knew its tyre costs in advance.

Rollout ran in three waves over 10 weeks, starting with the Delhi–Mundra corridor.

## The results

After 12 months on the programme:

| Metric | Before | After 12 months | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tyre cost per km | INR 0.52 | INR 0.43 | **-18%** |
| Tyres under-inflated >15% | 38% | 6% | -32 points |
| Tyre-related breakdowns per truck per quarter | 2.1 | 0.6 | **-71%** |
| Average retreads per casing | 1.1 | 2.4 | +118% |
| Tyres scrapped per 1,000 trucks per month | 96 | 58 | -40% |

Better pressure discipline also improved fuel efficiency by 2.3%, which saved NorthStar roughly INR 9 crore a year in diesel. This saving is on top of the tyre savings in the table.

"The biggest change is that we can now plan," said NorthStar's head of fleet maintenance. "Tyre cost is a line in the budget, not a surprise every month, and our trucks spend more time on the road."

## Why it worked

- **The incentives are aligned.** Roadgrip is paid per km, so casing life and uptime matter as much to us as they do to the fleet.
- **The data is shared.** Both teams watch the same dashboards and agree on what to act on each week.
- **Service happens at the depot.** Technicians who know the vehicles catch problems before they turn into breakdowns.
