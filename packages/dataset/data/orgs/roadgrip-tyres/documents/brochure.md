## Engineered in Chennai, built for every road

Roadgrip Tyres Ltd. makes tyres for almost everything that moves on Indian roads and in Indian mines. Three plants in Tamil Nadu and Gujarat produce 42 million tyres a year. We supply original-equipment fitment to more than 20 vehicle makers, and we run tyre programmes for some of the country's largest truck, bus and ride-hailing fleets.

Our compound lab and indoor pass-by noise track in Chennai let us develop tyres alongside our customers' platform teams, from the first concept sketch through to start of production.

## Product families

### EcoVolt EV Series — for electric cars and SUVs

Electric vehicles are heavier, deliver instant torque and have no engine noise to hide road roar. EcoVolt is designed for these conditions:

- **Low rolling resistance** silica tread compound, worth up to 6% more range in WLTP-equivalent testing
- **Acoustic foam insert** that cuts cabin noise by up to 4 dB
- **HL (high-load) construction** for battery-heavy SUVs
- Sizes from 15 to 20 inches, with aero sidewall options

### Terrain SUV & Passenger Range

Highway-terrain and all-terrain radials for hatchbacks, sedans and SUVs. They balance wet grip, tread life and ride comfort on mixed Indian road surfaces. Taxi-grade versions with reinforced sidewalls are available for fleet sedans and CNG vehicles.

### Moto Series — two-wheelers

Tubeless tyres for scooters, commuter motorcycles, premium motorcycles and adventure tourers, from 10 to 21 inches. Our low rolling resistance scooter compounds can add up to 8 km of range per charge on electric scooters.

### HaulMax TBR — truck and bus radials

Radials for long-haul, regional, city bus and tipper applications. Every HaulMax casing is rated for three retreads, and optional embedded sensors send pressure and temperature readings to FleetCare.

### Titan OTR — mining and construction

Radial and bias tyres for rigid dump trucks, wheel loaders, graders and excavators up to 57-inch rim. Cut- and heat-resistant compounds are built for Indian coal and iron ore pits. Because Titan OTR is made in India, lead times are measured in weeks rather than the months typical of imports.

## Services

**FleetCare Tyre Management.** Telematics-based pressure monitoring, scheduled rotation and alignment, retreading and depot technicians, all delivered as a managed service. FleetCare customers usually cut tyre cost per km by 12–20% within the first year.

**OEM Application Engineering.** A dedicated engineer is assigned to every new platform. Support covers tyre sizing, rolling-resistance and NVH targets, homologation testing and PPAP documentation.

**Replacement network.** More than 4,800 authorised outlets across India, plus distributors in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Kenya, Indonesia and Singapore.

Passenger, SUV, EV and two-wheeler tyres carry a **five-year warranty** against manufacturing defects. Truck and bus radials carry a three-year warranty that also covers one retread. Claims are settled at any authorised outlet within seven working days.
