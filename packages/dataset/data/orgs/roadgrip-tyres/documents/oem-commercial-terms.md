# OEM Commercial Terms — INTERNAL

*Roadgrip Tyres Ltd. Confidential: for the sales and key-account teams only. Fictional company, synthetic demo content.*

## 1. Purpose

This document sets out the standard commercial framework for original-equipment (OE) supply agreements with vehicle manufacturers. Any deviation needs the approvals listed in Section 8. Use this framework in every OEM quotation, whether it is for a new platform nomination, a plant expansion or a foreign OEM localising in India.

## 2. Engagement tiers

| Tier | Qualifying volume | Engineering support | Tooling |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Platform Partner** | 1 lakh+ tyres per annum per platform | Dedicated application engineer, joint validation | Co-funded 50:50 |
| **Preferred Supplier** | 25,000–1 lakh tyres per annum | Shared application engineer | OEM-funded, amortised over 3 years |
| **Approved Source** | Below 25,000 tyres per annum | On request | OEM-funded upfront |

New EV platforms qualify for Platform Partner treatment at 60,000 tyres per annum. This recognises the strategic value of EV fitment for the replacement market.

## 3. Pricing principles

- **Piece price** is quoted for each tyre size and specification. It is built from the standard costing sheet plus the target contribution margin for the tier: 22% for Platform Partner, 25% for Preferred Supplier and 28% for Approved Source.
- **Raw-material indexation** applies every quarter, using the published natural rubber, synthetic rubber and carbon black indices. The base is the quarter in which the quotation is issued.
- **Annual productivity give-back** is capped at 1.5% a year and is offered only to Platform Partners with a volume commitment of three years or more.
- **Launch pricing**: for the first six months after SOP we may offer up to 3% below the list OE price to win nomination. Recover it through indexation from month seven.

## 4. Payment and credit

- Standard terms: **45 days** from delivery, with consolidated monthly invoicing.
- Platform Partners with more than INR 50 crore of annual business may receive 60 days, subject to CFO approval.
- Foreign OEMs setting up in India receive a credit limit based on a parent-company guarantee or a standby letter of credit.
- Interest of 1.25% a month applies to overdue balances beyond 15 days of grace.

## 5. Supply commitments

- Dual-plant qualification (Chennai and Sanand) is included for Platform Partners, so supply continues if one plant is disrupted.
- Just-in-time or sequenced delivery is available within 500 km of a Roadgrip plant or warehouse. Outside that radius, a buffer stock of 10 days is held at an agreed 3PL location.
- Capacity is reserved against the OEM's 18-month rolling forecast. Commitments are firm for 3 months, variable by ±15% for months 4–6, and indicative after that.

## 6. Quality and warranty

- PPAP Level 3 submission and run-at-rate audit before SOP.
- The target is below 25 PPM, with 8D reporting within 10 working days of any complaint.
- OE warranty follows the vehicle warranty up to five years. Field-failure costs are shared on the basis of root-cause findings.

## 7. Signals that should trigger outreach

Key-account managers should open conversations as soon as any of the following is reported:

1. A new model launch or platform announcement, especially an EV platform
2. A new plant, capacity expansion or new assembly line
3. A foreign OEM's investment in India or localisation drive
4. PLI scheme approvals or state incentives awarded to an OEM

## 8. Approval matrix

| Deviation | Approver |
|---|---|
| Margin below tier target by up to 3 points | Sales Director |
| Margin below tier target by more than 3 points | CFO and Sales Director |
| Payment terms beyond 60 days | CFO |
| Tooling co-funding outside tier rules | Head of OE Business |
| Exclusive supply clauses | Managing Director |

## 9. Documentation checklist

- Signed NDA before sharing compound or construction data
- Quotation on the standard template with its indexation base quarter
- Capacity confirmation from Plant Planning
- Credit assessment from Finance
