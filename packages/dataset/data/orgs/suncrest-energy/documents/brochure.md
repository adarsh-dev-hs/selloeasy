## Grid compliance you can bank on

Every Suncrest inverter and PCS is tested to IEC 62109, IEC 61727 and IEC 62116 and meets Indian grid-connectivity requirements including low- and high-voltage ride-through. Export variants are certified for Gulf utility connection codes and the South African renewable grid code. VaultBESS is tested to UL 9540A. Lenders and owners' engineers receive full test reports on request.
