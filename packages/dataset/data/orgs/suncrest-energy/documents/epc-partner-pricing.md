# EPC Partner Pricing (Internal)

*Suncrest Energy Systems — INTERNAL. Do not share with end customers or non-certified partners.*

> Fictional demo data. Prices are illustrative and valid for the current financial year unless revised by Commercial.

## 1. Purpose

This note sets out how Suncrest prices inverters, storage and software to certified EPC partners, and how sales teams should structure bid support for tender-driven projects. It complements the EPC Partner Discount Policy.

## 2. Partner tiers

| Tier | Annual volume (Suncrest equipment) | Additional discount | Other benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Certified | Any | Base only | Design review, standard lead times |
| Silver | Above 50 MW per year | +3% | Priority allocation, quarterly business review |
| Gold | Above 200 MW per year | +5% | 90-day bid price lock, dedicated applications engineer, co-branded warranty |

Tier status is reviewed every six months on shipped (not ordered) volume.

## 3. Base partner discounts off list

- StringMax string inverters: 6%
- CentraSkid central stations: 6%
- HybriCharge hybrid inverters: 6%
- VaultBESS storage: 4%
- SunSight subscriptions: 10% on first-year fees when bundled with hardware

**Maximum total discount** without escalation is 14%. Anything above 14% requires Regional Sales Director approval; above 18% requires the VP Sales and CFO.

## 5. Bid support for tenders

When a partner is bidding for a utility-scale tender:

1. The account manager registers the opportunity in the CRM with tender reference, capacity and bid date.
2. Applications engineering produces a single-line diagram and energy yield estimate within five working days.
3. Gold partners receive a price lock for 90 days from the bid submission date. Silver partners receive 45 days.
4. If the partner is awarded the project, the locked price converts to a purchase order within 30 days of the letter of award, or the lock lapses.

Only one partner may hold a price lock on a given tender. First registration wins unless the customer has nominated a specific EPC.

## 6. Storage-linked projects

For projects that combine solar with a BESS component (increasingly common in firm and dispatchable power tenders):

- Offer the GridForm PCS firmware licence free for storage orders above 200 MWh, with VP Sales approval.
- Bundle two years of SunSight Optimise with BESS dispatch at no charge for orders above 100 MWh.
- Quote augmentation pricing for year 8–10 capacity top-ups upfront; lenders ask for it.

## 7. Payment terms

- Standard: 10% advance, 80% against dispatch, 10% on commissioning
- Gold partners: 5% advance, 85% against dispatch with a letter of credit, 10% on commissioning
- Delays beyond 60 days on the final tranche suspend price-lock eligibility on new bids

## 8. What not to do

- Do not quote partner prices directly to IPPs or end customers.
- Do not combine partner discounts with direct-sale promotional offers.
- Do not commit delivery dates without confirmation from supply-chain planning, especially for CentraSkid, which has a 14-week lead time.
