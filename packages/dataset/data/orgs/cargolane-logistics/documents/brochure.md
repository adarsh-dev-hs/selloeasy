# Cargolane Platform Overview

*Warehouse automation, WMS, TMS and cold-chain visibility from one accountable partner.*

## Who we are

Cargolane Logistics Tech is a supply chain technology company headquartered in Gurugram, India. We design, deploy and operate automated warehouses and the software that runs them. Our customers include D2C brands, e-commerce marketplaces, omnichannel retailers, FMCG companies, third-party logistics providers, port and ICD operators, and pharma and cold-chain distributors across India, the UAE, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and the United Kingdom.

Most supply chain teams today stitch together a robot vendor, a WMS vendor, a transport tool and a separate temperature-logging provider. When something breaks during a festive peak, nobody owns the problem. Cargolane was founded to be the single accountable partner for the whole flow, from inbound dock to customer doorstep.

## The platform

**LaneFlow WMS.** A cloud warehouse management system for single-client and multi-client sites. LaneFlow covers receiving, putaway, wave and zone picking, packing, returns, batch and expiry tracking, and kitting. It ships with connectors to more than 40 marketplaces, ERPs and carrier platforms, and natively orchestrates our robots and sorters, so there is no middleware layer to maintain.

**LaneRoute TMS.** A transport management system for primary, secondary and last-mile distribution. LaneRoute plans loads, optimises routes around time windows and vehicle constraints, runs carrier procurement and freight audit, and gives customers live tracking links. Typical customers reduce freight cost by 10 to 18 percent within six months.

**LaneBot AMR Fleet.** Goods-to-person autonomous mobile robots that bring shelving pods to ergonomic pick stations. Pickers move from roughly 90 to 300 order lines per hour, and new staff become productive in a single shift, which matters when seasonal hiring doubles your workforce.

**LaneSort.** Modular cross-belt and pouch sorters with dimensioning and weighing, from 4,000 to 20,000 parcels per hour, for sortation hubs, fulfilment centres and returns centres.

**LaneStack AS/RS.** High-density shuttle and pallet storage, including frozen-store variants rated to minus 25 degrees, delivered with certified integration partners.

**FrostEye Cold-Chain Monitoring.** Wireless sensors for cold rooms, reefer trucks, retail freezers and insulated shippers. FrostEye streams temperature and humidity data to a central control tower, raises alerts before an excursion becomes a loss, and produces GDP-compliant audit reports for pharma customers.
