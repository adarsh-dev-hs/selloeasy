# Robotics-as-a-Service Pricing (Internal)

**Confidential. For Cargolane sales, solutions and finance teams only. Do not share this document or its figures with customers or partners.**

## Purpose

This note sets out the standard commercial framework for Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS) deals involving LaneBot AMRs and, where bundled, LaneFlow WMS. It explains list prices, approved discount bands, peak-season flex terms and the approvals needed for exceptions.

## Standard RaaS subscription

| Fleet size (AMRs) | Monthly fee per robot (36-month term) |
|---|---|
| 20–59 | INR 52,000 |
| 60–149 | INR 46,000 |
| 150–299 | INR 41,000 |
| 300+ | INR 38,000 |

The monthly fee includes robot hardware, charging stations, preventive maintenance, spares, software updates, 24x7 remote monitoring and on-site response within 4 hours in our 12 service cities. Pick stations, pod shelving and site civil works are quoted separately as one-time charges, typically INR 3.5–6 lakh per pick station and INR 18,000 per pod.

LaneFlow WMS is mandatory for RaaS deployments. When bundled, apply the Growth or Enterprise software price with a bundle discount of up to 8% on software only.

## Contract terms

- Minimum term: 36 months. A 48-month term earns an additional 4% reduction on the robot fee.
- Refundable security deposit equal to two months of robot fees.
- Billing monthly in advance; net 30 payment terms.
- Annual escalation of 5% from month 13, capped at CPI plus 2% for enterprise accounts.
- Robot availability guarantee of 98.5%, measured monthly. Each 1% shortfall earns a 5% credit on that month's robot fees, capped at 20%.

## Peak-season flex robots

Customers on an active RaaS contract may add flex robots for festive and peak seasons:

- INR 65,000 per robot per month, minimum 2 months, maximum 50% of the base fleet.
- Flex requests must be confirmed 10 weeks before the start date so that robots can be reallocated from the pool.
- Flex robots for October–November are limited; prioritise customers with 150+ base robots and customers renewing within the next 12 months.

## Discount authority

| Discount on list robot fee | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to 5% | Account executive |
| 5–10% | Sales manager |
| 10–15% | VP Sales |
| Above 15% or deposit waiver | CFO |

Never quote below INR 36,000 per robot per month without written CFO approval. Pilots of fewer than 20 robots are priced at INR 58,000 per robot per month for a maximum of 6 months and must convert to a standard contract or be removed.

## Deal qualification checklist

Before proposing RaaS, confirm that the customer:

1. Has a committed site with a signed lease or ownership, and floor flatness suitable for AMRs.
2. Ships at least 8,000 order lines per day, or has a funded plan to reach that within 12 months.
3. Has named a project owner at Head of Warehousing level or above.
4. Accepts LaneFlow as the controlling WMS, or agrees to a scoped integration (additional INR 12–20 lakh).

## Positioning guidance

Lead with outcomes, not robot counts: throughput per picker, cost per order and the ability to scale for peaks without capex. Compare RaaS against the customer's current cost of temporary labour and overtime during festive periods; in most D2C sites the payback against a manual operation is 14 to 20 months. Recently funded brands and 3PLs opening new fulfilment centres are the most receptive, because RaaS keeps investor capital free for growth.
