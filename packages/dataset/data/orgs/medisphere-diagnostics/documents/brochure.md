# MediSphere Diagnostics — Solutions Brochure

*Faster results. Fewer vendors. Zero capex.*

MediSphere Diagnostics is a Bengaluru-based in-vitro diagnostics company. We help hospitals, diagnostic lab chains, home-collection providers and government health programmes build laboratories that report accurate results quickly, pass accreditation audits, and scale without large upfront investment. Our analyzers, kits and software are designed to work together, supported by a field service network that covers more than 180 cities in India and partner networks in the UAE, Singapore, Kenya and Bangladesh.

## What we offer

### SphereChem 800 — Clinical Chemistry
A fully automated chemistry analyzer processing up to 800 photometric tests per hour plus ISE. Sixty onboard reagent positions, closed-tube sampling and automatic rerun rules make it suitable for hospital central labs, processing labs of lab chains and medical colleges.

### SphereIA 240 — Immunoassay
A chemiluminescence immunoassay analyzer running 240 tests per hour. The menu covers thyroid, fertility, diabetes, cardiac markers (high-sensitivity troponin, NT-proBNP), tumour markers (PSA, CEA, CA-125, AFP) and infectious disease serology — the core menu for oncology and cardiac centres.

### SpherePOC Rapid Kits
Point-of-care kits for dengue, malaria, typhoid, HbA1c, CRP, troponin I and hepatitis, with a handheld reader that pushes results directly into SphereLIS. Ideal for emergency departments, ICUs, primary health centres and doorstep sample collection.

### SphereMDx RT-PCR Panels
RT-PCR kits for tuberculosis, hepatitis B and C viral load, HPV, respiratory viral panels and sexually transmitted infections. Validated on common open real-time PCR platforms, with optional automated extraction.

### SphereLIS Cloud — Laboratory Information System
A cloud LIS built for Indian and emerging-market labs:

- Barcode sample tracking from collection to report
- Instrument middleware and auto-verification rules
- NABL-ready QC dashboards, calibration logs and audit trails
- Home-collection app integration, B2B and hub-and-spoke workflows
- Report delivery by WhatsApp, email and patient portal
- HL7 and FHIR integration with hospital information systems

## How customers buy

**Reagent rental.** We place analyzers at no upfront cost. Customers commit to a monthly test volume and pay per reportable test, with installation, training and maintenance included. This model is popular with new hospitals, where capital is already stretched by construction and imaging equipment.

**LIS subscription.** SphereLIS is sold per site per month in Essentials, Professional and Enterprise tiers, so a three-lab chain and a fifty-lab network can both start quickly.

**Government tenders.** Our hub-and-spoke bundle combines hub-lab analyzers, PHC point-of-care kits and a state-level LIS dashboard, priced per test and aligned with standard tender formats.
