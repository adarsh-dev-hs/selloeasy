# Case Study: 40% Faster TAT for a Lab Chain

*How a regional diagnostic lab chain cut turnaround time by 40% and passed NABL audits at every site with MediSphere.*

## The customer

A regional diagnostic lab chain in South India (name withheld at the customer's request) operates 14 processing labs and around 320 collection points across three states, processing roughly 18,000 samples a day. Home sample collection had grown to more than a third of its volumes within two years, putting pressure on its labs to report faster and more consistently.

## The challenge

By early 2025 the chain faced three connected problems:

1. **A mixed analyzer fleet.** Its labs ran chemistry and immunoassay analyzers from four different manufacturers. Each had its own reagents, QC processes and service contract, and results were hard to compare across sites.
2. **Manual verification.** Pathologists and senior technologists manually reviewed almost every result before release. At peak hours, reports for routine panels were taking 9 to 11 hours from collection.
3. **Accreditation gaps.** Only five of its 14 labs held NABL accreditation. Calibration logs and QC records were kept partly on paper, and audits were stressful and time-consuming.

The chain's leadership wanted to reach same-day reporting for all routine tests, accredit every lab, and avoid a large capital outlay while it was also funding new collection centres.

## The solution

MediSphere proposed a phased consolidation on a reagent-rental model:

- **SphereChem 800 and SphereIA 240 analyzers** were placed at the nine highest-volume labs at zero capex, with smaller sites routed to hubs.
- **SphereLIS Cloud Professional** replaced three separate lab software systems. Instrument middleware connected every analyzer, and auto-verification rules were configured with the chain's pathologists for more than 180 parameters.
- **Home-collection integration** linked the chain's booking app to SphereLIS so that barcodes printed by phlebotomists at the patient's doorstep were recognised at the lab without re-entry.
- **QC dashboards and audit trails** in SphereLIS replaced paper logs, with automated Westgard rule alerts.
- MediSphere's application specialists trained 140 technologists over eight weeks, and the rollout was completed site by site without any day of lost testing.

## The results

Twelve months after go-live:

- **40% faster turnaround time.** Median time from collection to report for routine chemistry and thyroid panels fell from 9.5 hours to 5.7 hours.
- **72% auto-verification rate.** Nearly three in four results are now released automatically, freeing pathologists for complex cases.
- **All 14 labs NABL-accredited.** The nine remaining labs passed assessment within ten months, with no major non-conformities related to QC records.
- **18% lower cost per test.** Consolidating reagents and service contracts under one vendor, and eliminating duplicate software licences, reduced blended cost per reportable test.
- **98.9% analyzer uptime** across the year, above the contracted 98%.

## In the customer's words

"We used to treat every lab as its own island. Now we have one platform, one set of QC rules and one partner to call. Our doctors notice the difference because reports arrive the same afternoon," said the chain's Head of Laboratory Operations.

"Reagent rental meant we could standardise the whole network without taking capital away from our expansion plans," added its Chief Financial Officer.

The chain is now adding SphereMDx RT-PCR panels at its reference lab and piloting SpherePOC kits for doorstep HbA1c and CRP testing. It plans to extend SphereLIS Enterprise to six new labs opening over the next 18 months.
