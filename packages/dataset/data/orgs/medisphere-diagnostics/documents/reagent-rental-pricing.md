# Reagent Rental Pricing — INTERNAL

**Confidential. For MediSphere sales, pre-sales and finance teams only. Do not share with customers.**

This note sets out how we price reagent-rental deals and SphereLIS subscriptions, and the approvals required for discounts. It should be read together with the Pricing & Discount Authority policy.

## 1. How reagent rental works

Under reagent rental, MediSphere places analyzers at the customer site at zero capital cost. The customer commits to a minimum monthly test volume and pays a per-reportable-test rate that covers reagents, calibrators, controls, consumables, preventive maintenance, breakdown service and the depreciation of the placed analyzer. Standard term is 36 months, with a quarterly true-up if actual volumes fall below the commitment.

## 2. Minimum volume commitments

| Analyzer | Minimum committed volume | Typical site |
|---|---|---|
| SphereChem 800 | 2,500 chemistry tests / month | Hospital with 150+ beds, lab-chain processing lab |
| SphereIA 240 | 1,200 immunoassay tests / month | Hospital with 200+ beds, oncology or cardiac centre |
| SphereMDx (open PCR) | 400 reactions / month | Medical college, molecular reference lab |

Sites below these thresholds should be offered outright purchase or a shared hub arrangement rather than a placement.

## 3. List per-test rates (India, ex-GST)

| Test category | List rate (INR) | Floor rate (INR) |
|---|---|---|
| Routine chemistry (glucose, urea, creatinine, lipids) | 18–28 | 14 |
| Liver and renal panels (per parameter) | 22–30 | 16 |
| Thyroid (TSH, FT3, FT4) | 95–120 | 78 |
| Cardiac markers (hs-troponin, NT-proBNP) | 380–520 | 310 |
| Tumour markers (PSA, CEA, CA-125, AFP) | 260–340 | 210 |
| HbA1c | 110–140 | 90 |
| RT-PCR panels (per reaction) | 650–2,400 | per panel sheet |

Floor rates are absolute. No quote may go below the floor without written approval from the Head of Sales and Finance.

## 4. Discount authority

- **Up to 8%** off list: sales representative discretion.
- **8–15%**: Regional Sales Manager approval in the CRM.
- **Above 15%, or any government tender bid**: Head of Sales and Finance approval.

Discounts should be linked to volume, term length (48- or 60-month terms justify an additional 3–5%) or multi-site commitments, never offered unconditionally.

## 5. SphereLIS subscription pricing

| Plan | List price per site / month | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Essentials | INR 18,000 | Up to 300 samples/day, 10 instrument interfaces |
| Professional | INR 42,000 | Up to 2,000 samples/day, auto-verification, home-collection app integration |
| Enterprise | INR 30,000–35,000 | 10+ sites, annual contract, HIS integration, group analytics |

One-time implementation fees range from INR 1.5 lakh for Essentials to INR 12 lakh for Enterprise rollouts. Implementation may be waived for reagent-rental customers committing to more than 20,000 tests per month across sites.

## 6. Bundling guidance

- **New hospitals:** lead with reagent rental for chemistry and immunoassay, add SpherePOC for ICU and emergency, and SphereLIS Professional. Target go-live 45 days before patient admissions.
- **Lab chains after funding or acquisition:** propose platform consolidation across sites with SphereLIS Enterprise; model savings from reduced service contracts.
- **Government tenders:** use the Hub-and-Spoke Bundle price sheet; include operated-lab staffing only where the tender requires it.
- **International (UAE, Singapore, Kenya, Bangladesh):** add 12–18% to India list rates to cover freight, duties and partner margin, and confirm local registration status of each kit before quoting.

## 7. Deal hygiene

Every quote must record the committed volume, term, floor check and approver in the CRM. Quotes are valid for 60 days. Price revisions tied to currency movements may be applied annually with 90 days' notice.
