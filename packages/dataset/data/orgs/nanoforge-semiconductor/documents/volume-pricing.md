# Volume Pricing & Allocation (Internal)

**Confidential — for Nanoforge sales, FAE and supply-planning staff only. Do not share with customers or partners.**

This guide sets out standard price bands, discount authority and allocation rules for FY2026-27. All prices are in USD; INR quotes use the monthly treasury rate published on the first business day of each month. Quotes are valid for 30 days.

## 2. Discount authority

- **Account executive:** up to the published tier price.
- **Sales manager:** up to 18% below list, documented in CRM with competitor evidence.
- **CFO:** anything beyond 18%, any price lock longer than 24 months, and any most-favoured-customer clause.
- Never quote below the floor price in the pricing system; floors are recalculated quarterly with supply planning.

## 3. Strategic Allocation Tier

Available to accounts committing to more than 500k units a year across power products.

- 24- to 36-month take-or-pay commitment with quarterly rolling forecasts.
- Price bands reviewed every six months against SiC substrate cost indices.
- Eight weeks of buffer stock held in Hyderabad or Singapore.
- Allocation priority during shortages, after safety-critical automotive programmes already in production.
- Requires supply-planning sign-off before the proposal leaves sales.

Current strategic accounts receive quarterly allocation letters. New strategic accounts are capped at 15% of planned quarterly ForgeSiC output until qualification is complete.

Bundles: sites with five or more Nanoforge tools receive Forgesight at 20% off. Greenfield fabs receiving central or state incentives often run formal tenders; involve the bid desk as soon as a tender notice appears. Offer the free two-week yield baseline study only where there is a named line and ramp date.

## 5. Samples and evaluation

Up to 25 engineering samples per part number per account without approval. Evaluation boards are sold at cost. Anything above USD 5,000 in value needs a qualified opportunity in CRM with a named project and timeline.

## 6. Localization and domestic content

Several customers must meet domestic value-addition thresholds. Quote India-assembled ForgeSiC variants (suffix "-IN") where the customer needs local-content certificates; they carry a 3% premium to cover partner OSAT costs, which may be waived by a sales manager for accounts above 100k modules a year.

## 7. Escalation

Contact the sales manager for pricing questions, supply planning for allocation, and the compliance team for any export-control flag raised during quoting.
